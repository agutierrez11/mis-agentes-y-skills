# Huashu Design - High-Fidelity Artifacts & Presentations

Creates interactive pitch modals, executive slide decks, and visual territory telemetry for commercial applications.

## Components:
1. **Interactive Pitch Modals:** 1-click outreach templates with copy-to-clipboard and B2B MEDDIC qualification inputs.
2. **Spatial Telemetry:** Interactive GIS vector maps with circles proportional to contact density.
3. **Multi-Direction Critique:** Evaluating UI layout options across minimal, high-density, and dark-glass aesthetic philosophies.
